About KMU Digital Health Centre

The KMU Digital Health Centre Management System is a fully integrated electronic healthcare platform designed to replace paper-based medical records and streamline healthcare delivery for students, staff, visitors and healthcare professionals.

  • Student & Staff Identification
  • Electronic Medical Records
  • Real-Time Queue Monitoring
  • Laboratory & Pharmacy Integration
  • AI Powered Health Assistant
  • Real-Time Notifications
  • Automatic Reports & Analytics
